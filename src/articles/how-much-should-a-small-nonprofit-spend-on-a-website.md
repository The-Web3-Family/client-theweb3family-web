*From The Friday Letter, my weekly email for nonprofit founders.*

This week has been about figuring out my pricing model. The business is doing well, and my clients are happy, but originally I set the price where it was because I only wanted to take in serious candidates for my service.

The thing about providing a service is that not everyone is able to show up in order to receive it. And there is something interesting about paying for a service, and following up with the service to make sure you're getting your money's worth.

I know my Friday delivery model is very enjoyable for a client. They get updates, they move on with more ideas, and then they get updates.

However, I'm unsure if the barrier of entry is too high, or that the free work I do for everyone makes it so they don't follow up. My mentor kept saying, "You need to give out paid work, not free work." So I don't know... maybe she's right at this point, because I do a lot of free work, at the same quality level, but someone is still gonna go off and get charged the premium price for the same result. So... I think I need to let this go.

I will probably only provide a week's worth of work for free, and allow people to subscribe to maybe the $500 a month plan, or do a la carte. I don't know yet. I know the $3,000 a month tier is for those who really want that one on one time with me every week. But both the $3,000 and $500 plans get the weekly delivery. I was already able to structure three YouTube accounts, excluding mine, this week, and then I know I'll likely go back to working on the websites next.

Either way, I'm curious what everyone else thinks.
