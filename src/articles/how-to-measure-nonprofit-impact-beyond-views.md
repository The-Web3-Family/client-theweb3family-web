*From The Friday Letter, my weekly email for nonprofit founders.*

This week I set up the first phone line for one of the nonprofits I work with: 903-LOVEAID, for Love Aid Foundation. Call it and a short message tells you where to go next.

Watching it come to life taught me something I want every nonprofit founder to hear. Connection is the truest way to measure charity.

## Views are a scroll, a connection is a step

Many people measure their impact by views and impressions. Those numbers feel good. They also hide something.

A view means someone’s thumb moved past your post. It says very little about whether they cared.

Real impact begins when someone breaks out of their routine and takes a step toward you. They pick up the phone. They send a text. They sign up, reply, show up, or give. That step takes effort, and effort is the signal worth counting.

## Moments worth counting

Here are the steps I look for. Pick the ones that fit your mission:

- **A call or text** to your nonprofit’s number
- **A reply** to your email or newsletter
- **A newsletter signup** from your website
- **A donation**, even a small one
- **A volunteer signup** or event registration
- **A booked call** with you or your team
- **A message** asking for help or a referral

Each one is a person saying, “I want more of this.”

## How the phone line counts connections

For Love Aid Foundation, the phone line will do the counting. The plan is simple: count how many people call, which menu option they choose, and how long they stay connected. Calls stay private. The system counts them and records nothing about what people say.

Those totals can then feed straight into the website as proof of impact. A donor can see that real people reached real help this week. That tells a much stronger story than a view count ever could.

It also helps the nonprofit with funders. A phone line is a clear expense with a clear result, which is exactly what funders like to support.

## How to start counting this week

You can start small, with tools you already have:

1. **Choose one connection** that matters most to your mission, like calls, replies, or signups.
2. **Make it easy to take that step.** Put your number, your signup form, or your reply invitation on every post and every page.
3. **Count it every Friday.** A simple notebook or spreadsheet works. Write down the number each week.
4. **Share the number.** Put it in your newsletter or on your website. People trust what they can see.
5. **Ask what changed.** When the number goes up, look at what you did that week, and do more of it.

## Views still have a place

Views help people find you. But a view is the start of the path, never the destination. Measure your success by the moments people choose to come closer.
