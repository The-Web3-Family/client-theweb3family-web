*From The Friday Letter, my weekly email for nonprofit founders.*

This week we've gotten our first phone line up for one of our nonprofits, Love Aid Foundation: 903-LOVEAID.

It's great to see how connection is truly the way to measure charity.

Many people are measuring the impact of their efforts by the amount of views and impressions they get.

Frankly... someone scrolling past your content is not something that shows that someone is making a deliberate action towards your output.

When someone pushes past their monotony, and decides to take a step towards taking in more of your brand, and all that it produces, that's when you know you've truly hit the point of "impact."
