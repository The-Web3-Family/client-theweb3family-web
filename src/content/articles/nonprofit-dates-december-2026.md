*Nonprofit news, made simple. Here are the five dates small nonprofits should keep in mind between now and the new year, with one action for each.*

### December 1: Giving Tuesday, and your good standing

Americans gave a record $4.0 billion on Giving Tuesday last year. Before you share your donation link, check that your organization is in good standing with the IRS, the California Franchise Tax Board, and the California Attorney General. Under California's AB 488, online fundraising platforms check that standing too.

**Who it affects:** every nonprofit raising money online.<br>
**What to do this week:** look up your status with the IRS, the Franchise Tax Board, and the Attorney General's Registry of Charities.

Sources: <a href="https://www.givingtuesday.org/blog/2025-results/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">GivingTuesday 2025 results</a> · <a href="https://oag.ca.gov/charities/renewals" target="_blank" rel="noopener">California Attorney General, Registry renewals</a>

### December 11: a double federal deadline

Federal stopgap funding ends on December 11, and so does the pause on the federal grants rewrite.

**Who it affects:** every nonprofit with federal or pass-through funding.<br>
**What to do this week:** list every federal award on one page and plan for a possible funding gap.

Source: Public Law 119-103 (H.R. 6500), Section 157

### December 15: annual filings for July 31 year-ends

If your fiscal year ended July 31, your IRS Form 990 and your California filings (Form 199 and the RRF-1 report) are due December 15.

**Who it affects:** nonprofits whose fiscal year ended July 31.<br>
**What to do this week:** confirm your year-end date with your preparer.

Sources: <a href="https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/return-due-dates-for-exempt-organizations-annual-return" target="_blank" rel="noopener">IRS, return due dates for exempt organizations</a> · <a href="https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/misc/927.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">California FTB, due dates</a>

### December 31: $730,000 in LADWP grants

The Los Angeles Department of Water and Power opened its Community Partnership Outreach Grants on November 13: eight grants totaling up to $730,000. Applicants must be 501(c)(3) nonprofits located in the City of Los Angeles with at least two years of continuous status, and organizations outside the city can partner with an eligible lead applicant.

**Who it affects:** City of Los Angeles nonprofits.<br>
**What to do this week:** download the application and email it before December 31.

Source: <a href="https://www.ladwp.com/commercial-services/programs-and-rebates-commercial/community-partnership-outreach-grants-program" target="_blank" rel="noopener">LADWP Community Partnership Outreach Grants Program</a>

### January 1: new wage, new salary floor, new forms

California's minimum wage rises to $17.40 an hour. Salaried staff need at least $72,384 a year to stay exempt from overtime. New Attorney General charity forms also take effect.

**Who it affects:** every California nonprofit employer.<br>
**What to do this week:** check your salaries, and save the registration code letter the Attorney General mailed in August.

Sources: <a href="https://dir.ca.gov/DIRNews/2026/2026-66.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">California Department of Industrial Relations</a> · <a href="https://oag.ca.gov/charities/regs/registry-of-charities-and-fundraisers-online-filing-service" target="_blank" rel="noopener">California Attorney General, Online Filing Service</a>
