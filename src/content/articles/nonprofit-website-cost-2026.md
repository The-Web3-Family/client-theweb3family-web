<h2 id="cost">What does a small nonprofit website cost?</h2>

A small nonprofit site with up to 5 pages typically costs **$3,750 to $7,500** with a U.S. freelancer and **$6,500 to $13,000** with an agency. Costs rise with page count, donation tools and integrations.

Most nonprofits are small: **88% of U.S. nonprofits spend under $500,000 a year** (<a href="https://www.nonprofitimpactmatters.org/data/downloadable-charts/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Nonprofit Impact Matters</a>). This guide is written for them.

**Table 1: 2026 website build cost by nonprofit size**

| Nonprofit size | Pages | Freelancer | Agency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small charity | Up to 5 | $3,750–$7,500 | $6,500–$13,000 |
| Mid-size nonprofit | 5–15 | $6,700–$13,450 | $11,800–$23,600 |
| Large organization | 15–50 | $12,300–$24,550 | $21,550–$43,100 |

*Source: <a href="https://projectcostestimator.com/nonprofit-website-cost" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Project Cost Estimator, 2026</a>*

Freelancers and agencies usually charge nonprofits the same hourly rates as businesses, because the design and coding hours are the same. One real 2023 quote shared on Reddit came to **$7,500 for 85 hours**, about $88 an hour (<a href="https://www.reddit.com/r/nonprofit/comments/14w90oz/webmaster_needed_or_go_with_wix/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">r/nonprofit</a>).

<h2 id="monthly">Should you pay monthly or all at once?</h2>

Paying all at once suits organizations with a grant or a set project budget. Paying monthly suits organizations that want steady updates and predictable costs, with work spread across the year.

**Table 2: Your main options, year one**

| Option | Upfront | Monthly | Year-one total | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Do it yourself | $0–$400 | $8–$30 | $130–$1,500 | Volunteer-run groups under $50K |
| Freelancer | $3,750–$13,450 | $50–$300 | $5,100–$17,050 | Groups with their own writer and photos |
| Monthly studio plan | Varies | From about $495 | From about $5,940 | Busy teams that want steady progress |
| Agency | $15,000–$50,000+ | $125–$580 | $16,500–$57,000+ | Nonprofits with $1M+ budgets |

*Sources: <a href="https://www.hyperarts.com/nonprofit-website-cost/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">HyperArts, 2026</a>, <a href="https://projectcostestimator.com/nonprofit-website-cost" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Project Cost Estimator, 2026</a>, <a href="https://studiomesa.co/articles/how-much-does-a-nonprofit-website-cost-in-2026/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Studio Mesa, 2026</a>*

A website is also a fundraising tool. In 2025, **organic search brought 39% of nonprofit website visits**, about **1.6% of visitors donated**, and **37% of online giving arrived in December** (<a href="https://www.nonprofitpro.com/article/mr-benchmarks-2026-what-online-giving-growth-means-for-your-donor-file/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">M+R Benchmarks 2026 via NonProfit PRO</a>).

<h2 id="builders">Is Wix or Squarespace cheaper for nonprofits?</h2>

For the first two years, Wix is usually cheaper for verified 501(c)(3)s, thanks to a **70% TechSoup discount**. Squarespace offers **10% off your first payment** with the code NONPROFIT. Both return to full price at renewal.

**Table 3: 2026 website builder discounts for nonprofits**

| Builder | Regular price | Nonprofit offer | Donation fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wix (Core plan) | $29/month | 70% off a 2-year plan through TechSoup, plus a $34 admin fee and a free domain for 1 year | 0% platform fee + standard card processing (2.9% + $0.30) |
| Squarespace | $16–$29/month, billed yearly | 10% off first payment (code NONPROFIT) | 2% on the entry plan; 0% on Core and up |
| WordPress.com (Premium) | $8/month | Free and low-cost plans | 4% on the donations block |
| Webflow | $15–$25/month | 50% off the first year | Varies by tool |
| Google Sites | Free | Free with Google for Nonprofits | Use a separate donation tool |

*Sources: <a href="https://thenonprofitguide.com/guides/wix-for-nonprofits/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">The Nonprofit Guide, Nov 2026</a>, <a href="https://studiomesa.co/articles/how-much-does-a-nonprofit-website-cost-in-2026/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Studio Mesa, 2026</a>, <a href="https://website-builders.cybernews.com/for-non-profits/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Cybernews, 2026</a>*

**Tip:** put your renewal date on the calendar the day you sign up, so the full-price renewal arrives as a planned expense.

<h2 id="donations">Which donation platform keeps the most money?</h2>

On a $100 card gift, **Zeffy** and **Givebutter (with tips on)** pass the full $100 to your nonprofit, funded by optional donor tips. Stripe's nonprofit rate and PayPal's charity rate keep about **$97.50**.

**Table 4: What your nonprofit keeps from a $100 card gift**

| Platform | Fees | You keep |
|---|---|---|
| Zeffy | $0 (optional donor tips) | $100.00 |
| Givebutter (tips on) | $0 (optional donor tips) | $100.00 |
| PayPal (verified charity rate) | 1.99% + $0.49 | $97.52 |
| Stripe (nonprofit rate) | 2.2% + $0.30 | $97.50 |
| Donorbox Standard (on Stripe) | 2.95% + 2.2% + $0.30 | $94.55 |
| Givebutter (tips off, accounts opened after Sept 9, 2025) | 3% + 2.9% + $0.30 | $93.80 |

*Sources: <a href="https://www.zeffy.com/blog/paypal-donation-fees-for-nonprofits" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Zeffy, 2026</a>, <a href="https://nonprofitpoint.com/donorbox-vs-givebutter/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Nonprofit Point, 2026</a>, <a href="https://paymentform.io/blog/donation-platform-fees/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">PaymentForm, 2026</a>*

Monthly donors matter most: in 2025, **71% of monthly donors were still giving after a year**, compared with **24% of new one-time donors** (<a href="https://www.nonprofitpro.com/article/mr-benchmarks-2026-what-online-giving-growth-means-for-your-donor-file/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">M+R Benchmarks 2026</a>). Make recurring giving an easy option on your donate page.

<h2 id="hidden">What hidden costs show up after launch?</h2>

The build price is only part of the bill. Plan for these yearly costs:

- **Domain and hosting:** $60–$600 a year
- **Care plan or updates:** $500–$2,400 a year with a freelancer
- **Paid plugins:** $50–$500 a year each
- **Photography:** $300–$800 for a half-day shoot
- **Writing:** often extra, unless your team writes the pages
- **Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA):** cheapest when built in from day one

*Sources: <a href="https://www.hyperarts.com/nonprofit-website-cost/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">HyperArts, 2026</a>, <a href="https://studiomesa.co/articles/how-much-does-a-nonprofit-website-cost-in-2026/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Studio Mesa, 2026</a>, <a href="https://pixellighthouse.com/nonprofit-website-faq/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Pixel Lighthouse</a>*

<h2 id="grants">What free tech grants are still available in 2026?</h2>

Google, TechSoup and Canva still offer major free programs. Microsoft changed its grants in July 2025, so double-check older advice.

- **Google for Nonprofits:** free Google Workspace and **up to $10,000 a month in Google Ad Grants** for search ads. To keep the grant, your ads need at least a 5% click-through rate each month.
- **TechSoup:** unlocks discounts like 70% off Wix and donated software.
- **Canva for Nonprofits:** free Canva Pro for up to 50 people.
- **Microsoft:** on **July 1, 2025**, Microsoft ended its free Business Premium grant. Business Basic is still free for up to 300 users, Business Premium is now **$5.50 per user per month**, and eligible nonprofits get **$2,000 a year in Azure credits**.

*Sources: <a href="https://grantsights.com/blog/microsoft-nonprofit-grants" target="_blank" rel="noopener">GrantSights, 2026</a>, <a href="https://reachrightstudios.com/blog/microsoft-365-for-nonprofits/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Reach Right Studios, 2026</a>*

Good to know: these grants cover software and ads. Paying a web designer usually comes from operating funds or a capacity-building grant.

<h2 id="keys">How do you keep your website when a volunteer leaves?</h2>

Keep the keys in your organization's name. A five-minute setup today saves a full rebuild later.

1. Register your domain under the organization's email, with auto-renew on.
2. Give at least two board or staff members admin access.
3. Store logins in a shared password manager.
4. Keep copies of every photo, logo and page in your organization's Google Drive.
